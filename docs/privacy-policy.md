# ClassicMail Privacy Policy

_Effective 24 October 2026_

ClassicMail is a desktop mail, calendar, contacts and tasks program for Windows, made by Duvz
Software (Kenny Green). This policy explains what ClassicMail does with your information. In short:
**your data stays between you, Google and your own computer.** Duvz Software runs no ClassicMail
servers, has no user accounts and never receives your data.

## The Google data ClassicMail uses, and why

When you connect a Google account, you choose to give ClassicMail access to:

| Data | What ClassicMail does with it |
|---|---|
| **Gmail** (read, send, organize and delete mail; Gmail filters) | Shows your mail in folders, lets you read, write, reply, forward, send, move, flag, delete and search it, and keeps Outlook-style Rules in sync as Gmail filters. |
| **Google Calendar** | Shows and edits your calendars, appointments and meetings, sends meeting invitations and replies, and shows reminders. |
| **Google Contacts** (including "other contacts", read-only) | Shows and edits your contacts and groups, and suggests addresses as you type. |
| **Google Tasks** | Shows and edits your tasks and To-Do Bar. |
| **Basic profile** (name, e-mail address) | Identifies the account you signed in with. |

ClassicMail uses this data only to provide the features you see in the program. It does not use
it for advertising, does not sell it, does not use it to judge creditworthiness, and does not use
it to train artificial-intelligence models.

## Where your data is kept

- ClassicMail downloads your mail, calendar, contacts and tasks to your computer so it can work
  fast and offline. They are stored in the ClassicMail data folder of your Windows user profile.
  Protect that folder like your other files, for example with Windows device encryption
  (BitLocker).
- Your Google sign-in is stored in Windows Credential Manager, never in a plain file. The same
  goes for AI API keys you enter.
- Attachments and files are saved only where you choose.
- ClassicMail keeps a diagnostic log on your computer to help fix problems. It records technical
  events (such as error codes and counts), not the content of your mail, and is never sent anywhere.

## Who your data is shared with

- **Google.** ClassicMail exchanges data directly with Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to
  carry out what you do: download, send, change or delete your mail, events, contacts and tasks.
- **The AI provider you choose, only if you use the AI features.** Translate, Reply with AI and AI
  Review send the text they work on (and, for AI Review, calendar or task details it looks up) to
  Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, using **your own API key**, directly from your computer.
  Nothing is sent until you use an AI command for the first time and agree to a notice explaining
  this, and nothing is sent unless you use an AI command. That provider handles the text under its
  own terms; note that with a free Gemini key, Google may use what it receives to improve its
  products. You can turn the AI features off at any time in Tools › Options › AI.
- **Senders' servers, only if you allow it.** Pictures in mail that are stored on the sender's
  servers are not downloaded unless you choose to show them (or the sender is on your Safe Senders
  list).
- **No one else.** Duvz Software never receives, reads or stores your data. ClassicMail contains no
  analytics, tracking or advertising.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

ClassicMail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will
adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data stays on your computer until you remove it. **Account Settings › Remove** deletes
  everything ClassicMail stored for that account from your computer and withdraws its access to your
  Google account.
- Uninstalling ClassicMail with **Delete app data** ticked removes all of its data from your
  computer.
- You can also withdraw ClassicMail's access at any time at
  <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
- Removing data from your computer does not delete anything from your Google account itself.

## Children

ClassicMail is not directed at children under 16.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in ClassicMail (Help › Privacy Policy) with
a new effective date.
